        05 — Rollout

        A careful move from the old books.

        Changing systems in the middle of a session is a serious undertaking. These are ours.

        1. i.

          A migration plan before go-live

          We map your existing student, academic and finance records into the SchoolClerk structure before your team starts, so the first day isn’t a data-entry day.

        2. ii.

          Access that respects school and role

          Each school works at its own address with its own records. Staff see the modules and classes their role allows — a teacher sees their classes, the bursar sees finance.

        3. iii.

          Rollout support for every office

          Guided onboarding sets up your session, classes, fees and staff. We stay with your team while each office moves across.
