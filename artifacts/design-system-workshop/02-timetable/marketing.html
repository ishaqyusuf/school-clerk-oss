Rollout Our commitments

Going live is planned with you, not handed to you.

Three commitments that hold for every school, whatever its size or the modules it starts with.

Before go-live

A migration plan for your records

We map your existing student, academic and finance records into the SchoolClerk structure before your team starts using it, so day one begins with your data in place.

At go-live

Tenant- and role-aware access

Your school runs at its own address, kept apart from every other school. Inside it, each person sees what their role allows — finance for the bursar, classes for teachers.

After go-live

Rollout support for your staff

Guided onboarding for administrators and staff, with more modules switched on when your operations are ready for them — not before.
