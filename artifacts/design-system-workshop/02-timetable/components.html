Direction 02 · School Clerk design system

Timetable — everything in its period.

A precise, keyboard-first workspace for the bursar and registrar who live in School Clerk all day. Cool white surfaces and near-navy ink, cobalt for action, and visible grid lines in place of shadows. Identifiers, amounts and dates sit in a monospaced face so columns line up; a highlighter yellow only ever marks where you are — the keyboard cursor, the current week, a search hit — and always carries dark text.

11

Do / don’t

Six rules that keep Timetable from drifting back to the old monochrome, icon-rail dashboard.

Do
Don’t
01Navigate with the dark module bar and a labelled sub-nav that shows every section of the module.
01Hide navigation in an icon rail that only explains itself on hover.
02Separate surfaces with 1px grid lines; keep shadows for things that float.
02Float soft grey cards with drop shadows on a grey page.
03Set IDs, amounts and dates in Plex Mono with tabular numerals, at full contrast.
03Set data in small, light-grey sans that blurs down a column.
04Use square tags with a 2px status bar and readable text.
04Use monochrome badges, or pills where colour is the only signal.
05Reserve yellow for position: cursor row, now marker, search hit, active module — always with dark text.
05Use yellow for buttons, warnings or decoration.
06Show the keyboard path: kbd hints in buttons and footers, ⌘K, ⌘↵, ↑ ↓ X.
06Put row actions only behind hover, where keyboards and touch can’t find them.
