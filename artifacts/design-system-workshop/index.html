Three directions for a School Clerk design system

Today's dashboard is adapted from Midday: monochrome, a hover-expanding icon rail and square badges. Each direction below is a complete system on standard shadcn/ui. Every direction covers marketing, signup and onboarding, the dashboard shell and tables, forms with side sheets and dialogs, and the website generator. Each direction has its own tokens and silhouette. Sample content is identical everywhere (Kingsway Academy, 2026/2027 First Term), so you're comparing design, not copy.

  • Compare by surfaceOpen the same surface in all three directions, for example Dashboard 01, 02 and 03. Use the bar at the bottom-left of each page to switch surface or theme.
  • Everything works locallyForms validate, sheets and dialogs open, tables filter and select, the website editor live-previews. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Mix and match is fineYou can choose one direction whole, or combine parts, e.g. "01 marketing, 02 tables, 03 onboarding". A favourite is not a build decision.

Every direction keeps the canonical School Clerk mark, the tenant-domain model (kingsway.school-clerk.com and dashboard.kingsway.school-clerk.com) and the rule that school websites use the school's own theme, never the platform palette (ADR-0020). Tokens use shadcn variable names plus a few extras (--success, --warning, --info, --highlight, fonts and density), so the chosen direction drops straight into packages/ui/src/styles/globals.css.

Suggested starting point: 01 Registry, with two borrowings

Registry keeps the evergreen identity you already like and makes it unmistakably School Clerk rather than Midday: an inset evergreen frame, ledger tables, numbered sheets and a seal moment. Its main cost is density. Borrow 02's keyboard cursor, ⌘K palette and compact row density for finance and other heavy tables. Borrow 03's bottom tab bar and checklist-home onboarding for phones, where teachers and parents will mostly be. If you'd rather go bolder, 02 is the strongest for daily operations; 03 is the most welcoming.
