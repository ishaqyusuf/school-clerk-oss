        Switching systems without the scramble

        Three things we commit to before, during and after your school goes live.

        1. Before go-live

          A migration plan for your records

          We map your existing student, academic and finance records into SchoolClerk with you, and agree what moves when.

        2. Every day

          Access that respects your school

          Each school keeps its own workspace and configuration, and every staff member sees only what their role allows.

        3. Through rollout

          Support while each office settles in

          Admissions, the bursary and teachers can start one at a time, with guided setup at a pace your school chooses.
